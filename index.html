How the numbers are worked out
  • Material cost per unit = sum of quantity × price over all BOM items.
  • Monthly production = production per quarter ÷ 3. Any remainder goes to the first month of the quarter, so each quarter adds up exactly.
  • Safety stock = production per quarter ÷ 13 weeks × safety weeks, rounded up to whole sets. It arrives with the first order and is still on hand at the end of the 12 months.
  • Purchases = production + ending stock − opening stock. The opening stock is taken as 0 (a new line).
  • Latest PO date = first day of the month that uses the material − lead time. A date on a Saturday or Sunday moves back to the Friday before.
  • A items: items sorted by spend; an item is A while the items before it make up less than 80% of the budget.
  • Not included: scrap or yield loss, minimum order quantities, freight, insurance, import duty and price breaks.
